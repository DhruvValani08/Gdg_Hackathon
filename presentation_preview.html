⚠️ The Problem 02 / 12

The High-Volume OPD & Referral Bottleneck

Public tertiary hospitals in India face dual systemic challenges daily.

🚨 The OPD Language Divide

  • 5,000+ walk-in patients daily at district civil hospitals.
  • Doctors have <2 minutes per consultation.
  • Patients speak regional dialects and miss crucial details (e.g. chronic BP meds, allergies).

🚑 The Rural Referral Blindspot

  • 108 Ambulances transfer critical cases from village PHCs.
  • Paper referral chits get lost, smudged, or lack critical timestamps.
  • Receiving ER doctors miss exact Anti-Snake Venom (ASV) dosage times or in-transit vomiting.
🔄 Clinical Architecture 03 / 12

End-to-End Connected Clinical Workflow

Seamless transition of structured data across 4 dedicated clinical roles.

1. Patient Intake

Voice input + "Sahai Mitra" chat assistant auto-fills history & generates Token ID.

2. Staff Triage

Sister Anjali reviews AI summaries & routes to Cardiology/Medicine/Ortho/Emergency.

3. Doctor Suite

Dr. Mehta reviews isolated queue, establishes diagnosis & signs official digital Rx.

4. Health Camps

Offline village screening & outbound 108 ambulance dispatch dossiers.

⛺ Persona 4 08 / 12

🚑 Outbound 108 Referral Dossiers

🛡️ Responsible AI 09 / 12

Safety-First Clinical Guardrails

Strict boundaries ensuring AI augments physicians without prescribing.

🚫 Strict Non-Diagnostic Boundary

Gemma NEVER predicts diseases or suggests medication doses. Only human doctors prescribe.

🚨 Deterministic Emergency Override

Hardcoded red-flag rules trigger instant alerts for chest pain, snakebites, and trauma.

👨‍⚕️ Human Doctor in the Loop

Every prescription and diagnosis must be authorized and signed by an attending physician.

🔒 Complete Data Privacy

Patient health records stay strictly inside hospital infrastructure.
